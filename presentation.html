The $500M Blind Spot

Training a frontier LLM costs $100–500M, occupies thousands of GPUs for months, and processes trillions of tokens. Yet practitioners monitor this investment through a single signal: the training loss.

  • Loss can decrease while model structure degrades (memorization)
  • Loss is scale-dependent — incomparable across model sizes
  • No signal for when to decay LR — arbitrary fixed schedules
  • No early warning for structural pathologies

Core Idea: Spectral Thermodynamics

Two Metrics, Complete Picture

SR/d — Stable Rank / Hidden Dim

Measures compression progress. How much of the layer's capacity is actually used.

SR = ||W||²F / σ²1 = exp(HRényi-2)

α — Power-Law Exponent

Measures structural quality. Whether the layer has developed organized, heavy-tailed features.

Not Analogy — Identity

The stable rank is the exponential of Rényi-2 entropy of the singular-value distribution. Spectral monitoring = direct measurement of thermodynamic state.
