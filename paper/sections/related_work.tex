\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
%=============================================================================

\paragraph{Statistical physics of neural networks.}
The application of statistical mechanics to learning systems has a long history~\citep{engel_van_den_broek_2001}, but recent work has established \emph{quantitative} correspondences rather than loose analogies.
\citet{sadrtdinov2025} proved that SGD with finite learning rate minimizes free energy $\free = \intE - \temp\entropy$, not loss alone, and \citet{pvnkt_ijcai2026} showed that scale-invariant networks with weight decay exactly satisfy the ideal gas law $\press\vol = Nk\temp$.
\citet{liu_tegmark2025} derived three thermodynamic laws for LLM training and showed the optimal schedule corresponds to a quasi-static process, though experiments were limited to GPT-2-small (124M).
\citet{liu_ziyin_neurips2025} established entropic force theory, proving that SGD stochasticity systematically breaks continuous parameter symmetries.
\citet{okanohara2026} proved the Epistemic Speed Limit---an algorithm-independent lower bound on entropy production for any learning process, analogous to Landauer's principle.
\citet{seroussi2023speed} derived training speed limits via Wasserstein-2 distance bounds.
All of these results were verified only at small scale.
Our work provides the first large-scale (190M--13B) empirical validation and extends the framework to multi-stage pretraining, which none of these papers address.

\paragraph{Phase transitions and glass dynamics in learning.}
\citet{grokking_glass_neurips2025} demonstrated that grokking is a glass relaxation process: memorization corresponds to rapid quenching into a disordered state, and generalization to slow annealing toward an ordered state.
They developed the WanD optimizer to eliminate grokking entirely.
\citet{barney_spin_glass2024} mapped untrained networks to Sherrington-Kirkpatrick spin glasses and showed that training creates hidden order.
\citet{winter_janssen2025} provided an important counterpoint: DNN training shares features with structural glass dynamics but is not a perfect glass (no diverging relaxation times, no caging).
\citet{phase_transitions_on_model} reformulated transformers as O(N) spin models with two phase transitions.
\citet{scaling_collapse_icml2025} discovered that loss curves from different-scale compute-optimal models collapse onto a single universal curve under renormalization, and \citet{rg_for_dnns2025} established a renormalization group framework for weakly nonlinear networks.
\citet{spring_block_prl2025} derived a spring-block chain model for layerwise feature learning, published in \emph{Physical Review Letters}.
Our contribution applies glass relaxation theory specifically to the mid-training phase transition, with quantitative KWW fits at billion-parameter scale.

\paragraph{Learning rate schedules and WSD theory.}
\citet{wsd_schedule} introduced the river-valley landscape conjecture to explain WSD: the stable phase drives progress along flat directions while decay eliminates oscillations in sharp directions.
\citet{mpemba_llm} connected WSD to the Mpemba effect (hotter systems cool faster) but provided no empirical validation.
\citet{bordelon_schedules2026} and \citet{li_schedules2026} independently derived WSD optimality from optimal control theory, finding a sharp phase transition between ``easy-task'' (power decay optimal) and ``hard-task'' (WSD optimal) regimes.
\citet{annealing_scaling_law2024} proposed an empirical scaling law decomposing training into power-law scaling plus annealing reduction, and \citet{annealing_scaling_aaai2026} studied transferability of annealing strategies across Dense and MoE models.
\citet{kalra_curvature2026} measured progressive sharpening and Edge of Stability at 7B scale (OLMo-2), providing the first large-scale loss landscape curvature measurements without thermodynamic interpretation.
Our work differs by providing the first \emph{thermodynamic} explanation of WSD superiority (entropy production), rather than geometric (river-valley) or control-theoretic (optimal control) accounts.

\paragraph{Multi-stage pretraining and data curriculum.}
The convergence of the industry on multi-stage training~\citep{olmo2, deepseekv3, llama3, llama4} has created an urgent need for principled guidance on stage transitions.
OLMo~2 uses a two-stage recipe: 3.9T tokens of web data followed by 843B tokens of curated data~\citep{olmo2}.
DeepSeek~V3 employs a similar pattern with domain-reweighted mid-training~\citep{deepseekv3}.
\citet{cpt_dynamics2025} derived a continual pretraining scaling law that decouples distribution shift from LR annealing, enabling loss prediction across schedules.
The overtraining fragility phenomenon~\citep{overtraining_fragile2025} shows that models trained far beyond the Chinchilla point become harder to fine-tune, but the mechanism remains unexplained.
No prior work provides a thermodynamic account of why multi-stage training works, how long each stage should last, or what happens to the model's internal state at stage boundaries.
Our glass annealing theory directly addresses these questions.
